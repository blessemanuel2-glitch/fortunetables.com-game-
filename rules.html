FORTUNE TABLES • WEB EDITION

HOW TO PLAY

PLAY YOUR HAND. OWN YOUR FORTUNE.

Fortune Tables is a live web-based property, money and social strategy game. One player is the Banker; everyone else competes to finish with the strongest fortune.

1. SET UP

One person creates the table as Banker. Everyone else joins using the table code and chooses a symbol. Each player begins with 50 TABLES.

2. THE BANKER

The Banker controls the digital Bank, property approvals, transfers, auctions, rent, debt, jail, alerts, theft catches and the final winner.

3. YOUR TURN

Roll the digital die, move automatically, then resolve the space you land on. Turns rotate through active players automatically.

4. PROPERTIES

Bank-owned properties can be requested for purchase. The Banker can ACCEPT or DECLINE. Owned properties generate rent and count toward net worth.

5. RENT + DEBT

Pay required rent and fees through the website. Debt lowers your final fortune. Overdue property obligations may lead to repossession.

6. DEALS + AUCTIONS

Players can negotiate, transfer assets and bid in live auctions. A deal is final only after the digital transaction is recorded.

SPECIAL BOARD SPACES

Landing on one of these spaces opens its matching digital deck.

7. STEAL

Draw a Steal Action card and follow it. Normal property theft gives the Banker 20 seconds to catch you. If you escape, you keep the property. If caught, the property returns and you go to Fortune Jail.

8. JACKPOT / BANK HIJACK

Draw a Hijack card to receive a one-time Bank code. Enter it in the Bank Terminal for the listed TABLES reward. The rare JACKPOT-777 pays +25 TABLES.

9. MARKET CRASH

Draw a Market Crash card and apply its effect. These events can change balances, debt, rent, property values or the entire market.

10. CONFESSION

Draw a Confession card, read it aloud and answer truthfully. The table may allow a skip if everyone agrees.

11. FORTUNE JAIL

You can be frozen by board events, penalties or getting caught stealing. Pay the displayed bail or receive a Banker release before using locked game actions.

12. CARD OVERRIDE

If a card changes a normal rule, the card wins. Example: a Steal card may shorten the Banker timer or change bail.

HOW TO WIN

NET WORTH = CASH + PROPERTY VALUE − DEBT

The Banker calls the final round. Every active player gets one last turn, then the player with the highest net worth is crowned the FORTUNE TABLES WINNER 💎.
